About Wishlistz

1. What is Wishlistz?
Wishlistz is a customer‑centric platform designed to simplify shopping, order tracking, and support.

Established in 2024, Wishlistz focuses on blending technology with personalized shopping experiences.

3. What services does Wishlistz provide?
From order tracking and returns to subscription management and loyalty rewards, Wishlistz covers the full shopping journey.

Through responsive support, transparent policies, and a rewards system that values loyal customers.

A seamless integration of profile management, chatbot assistance, and personalized recommendations.

Simply sign up via the Profile section and choose a subscription plan that fits your needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I track my order?
Use the 'Track Order' option in the Services section to view real‑time updates.

2. How do I return an item?
Select 'Return Order' and follow the guided steps. Ensure the item is unused and within the return window.

3. How do I contact support?
Use 'Raise Tickets' for technical issues or 'Contact Us' for general inquiries. Our team responds within 24 hours.

4. What payment methods are accepted?
We support Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, and Wallets. Check 'Payment History' for details of past transactions.

5. How do I know if my refund has been processed?
Go to 'Refund Status' in Services. Refunds typically take 5–7 business days depending on your bank.

6. Can I change my subscription plan?
Yes, under 'Profile' → 'Subscription Plan'. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.

7. How do I update my saved addresses or cards?
Navigate to 'Profile' → 'Account Information'. You can add, edit, or remove saved addresses and payment cards.
